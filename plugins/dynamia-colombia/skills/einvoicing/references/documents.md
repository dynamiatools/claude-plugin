# Documents and obligations

Read from Resolución 000165 de 2023 on the normograma (articles now compiled in 000227 de 2025). Confirm the current text before relying on a detail.

## Who must issue an electronic sales invoice (arts. 7 to 9)
Responsables de IVA, responsables del impuesto nacional al consumo, merchants, professionals and service providers, agricultural and livestock sellers, and taxpayers of the SIMPLE regime, with exceptions. SIMPLE taxpayers may invoice electronically from their registration, use the electronic equivalent document when its annex exists, or use the technical-problem rule for up to two months.
**Not obliged (art. 8)** includes banks and financial entities, certain natural persons, urban and metropolitan transport providers, some employees and pensioners, those who only sell goods excluded or not taxed below a limit expressed in UVT (3.500 UVT in the text read), foreign digital-service providers without Colombian tax residence, and some community action boards. If a non-obliged person chooses to invoice, it becomes obliged for tax purposes.

## Documents
| Document | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Factura electrónica de venta** | Needs validación previa. Content (art. 11): express denomination, seller and buyer data, numbering, date and time, payment form and means, itemized taxes (including health-related ones), digital signature, **CUFE**, URL of the **QR code**, manufacturer and software data, structure of the annex |
| **Notas crédito y débito** | Electronic, with CUDE and validación previa; reference the original invoice |
| **Documento equivalente electrónico** (arts. 15 to 20, annex v1.0) | Replaces equivalent documents of the DUR 1625: common content (denomination, seller, itemized taxes, consecutive number, generation and issue date and time, total, software, **CUDE**, QR, signature). **POS ticket**: number, range and validity of the authorization, and buyer identification when the buyer needs costs or deductible taxes. Specific types: cinema ticket, transport ticket, extracts, airline ticket, games of chance, tolls, stock exchange, domiciliary public services, public shows (with PULEP code) |
| **Notas de ajuste** | Correct arithmetic or content errors of an equivalent electronic document |
| **Factura de talonario o papel** | Valid only when the invoicer has a technical problem; if generated by a computer system it must carry QR and software data |
| **Factura sin validación previa** | Valid only when the DIAN cannot validate for its own technical reasons; transmit it within 48 hours after the problem is solved; the date of issue is the generation date |
| **Documento soporte** | Supports purchases from sellers not obliged to invoice (compiled in Título 5, capítulo 2 of 000227) |
| **Nómina electrónica** | Electronic payroll support (compiled in Título 5, capítulo 3) |
| **RADIAN** | Events and registry for invoices that are traded as títulos valores (Título 5, capítulo 4) |
| Factura de exportación, contingencia, others | Types defined in the annex code lists; see the annex |

## Validity
- Expedition means generation, transmission, validation and delivery to the buyer, at the moment of the operation (art. 5). An invoice counts as issued only when validated and delivered (art. 11 numeral 6; DIAN Concepto 5480 de 2025 repeats it).
- The buyer receives the electronic format plus the "Documento validado por la Dian" response, both inside the electronic container.
- Numbering: prefix of up to four alphanumeric characters, consecutive number, and the validity of the numbering authorization from the DIAN service. The POS ticket also needs authorized number, range and validity.
- Environments: *producción en habilitación* (tests evaluated by the DIAN) and *producción en operación*.
