# Obligated parties and formats (as read on 2026-10-23)

Source: Resolución 000162 de 2023 (normograma; many articles now compiled in 000227 de 2025) and Resolución 000233 de 2025. The text read was truncated after article 20, so formats not listed here exist: take the full list from the annexes of the año gravable. Thresholds and formats **change by year**: verify.

## Who must file (art. 1 of Res. 162 de 2023, thresholds in UVT of the reported year or the previous one)
| Party | Condition |
|---|---|
| Personas naturales y asimiladas | gross income above 11.800 UVT **and** capital/non-labor income above 2.400 UVT in the reported year |
| Personas naturales del SIMPLE | gross income above 11.800 UVT |
| Personas jurídicas y asimiladas | gross income above 2.400 UVT |
| Retenedores / autorretenedores (renta, IVA, timbre) | the year's obligation, no threshold (modified by Res. 188 de 2024) |
| Socios that did not report share sales | sale of 5.000 UVT or more, not listed on the stock exchange |
| Without a threshold | financial entities and those supervised by the Superfinanciera, stock exchanges and brokers, permanent establishments, consortia and joint ventures, public entities, Cámaras de Comercio, notaries, invoicers, municipalities and departments, carbon tax payers, entities issuing certificates for tax benefits, and others listed |
| New from AG 2026 (Res. 233 de 2025) | providers of digital assets with operations in the country and gross income above 1.400 UVT; entities that certify tax benefits (UPME, APC-Colombia, ANLA, CAR...) |
Exclusions: taxpayers in RUT cancellation; the sale of the dwelling is not counted in gross income for the threshold. Horizontal property does not report the administration-fee format 1007 (Res. 233).

## Third-party identification (Res. 162 de 2023)
- Foreign third parties: their tax id of the country of residence, or the codes `444444001` to `444449999` with document type `43` when they have none.
- Unidentifiable sellers: type `43`, number `888888888`, name "vendedor desconocido".
- Negative balances keep their sign. Payments or withholdings reported in a special format are not repeated in 1001.

## Calendar AG 2025 (DIAN comunicado 053 de 2026, filed in 2026)
Grandes contribuyentes: 28 April to 13 May 2026. Personas jurídicas y naturales: 14 May to 12 June 2026. Consolidated financial statements and share sellers: 30 June 2026. Alcaldías y distritos (ICA): 31 August 2026. Exact day depends on the filer type and the last digit(s) of the NIT, per the calendar linked in the comunicado. A draft (proyecto) of May 2026 proposed extending some formats (2820 to 2840) to 31 August 2026: a draft, confirm the final resolution. The DIAN has issued exceptional calendars before (AG 2024), so recheck every year.
